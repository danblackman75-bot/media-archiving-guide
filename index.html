Tips for doing the work

  1. Handle gently. Clean, dry hands or cotton gloves; hold photos and film by the edges.
  2. Dust, don't wipe. Brush or blow off dust; a soft microfiber cloth helps. No water or cleaners. Wipe the scanner glass each session.
  3. Feeder for healthy prints only. Torn, curled, thick or stuck photos go on the flatbed or in the FastFoto's carrier sheet.
  4. Name folders by year and event. For example Photos/1975/1975-07 Lake trip/ with files like 1975-07_Lake-trip_001.tif.
  5. Write down who and where. The FastFoto saves the backs of photos; add names in digiKam or Google Photos.
  6. Back up after every session. Copy new files to the external drive; online backup runs on its own.
  7. Keep the originals. Store them in acid-free boxes somewhere cool and dry.

Doing it yourself

  • Cheaper per photo once past a few hundred
  • Originals never leave the house
  • He keeps a new PC and scanner afterward
  • Can add names and stories as he goes

Sending it out

  • No time or learning needed
  • Best for a handful of tapes, film reels, or damaged tapes
  • Costs add up fast with thousands of photos
  • Originals are shipped away for weeks
Good middle ground: scan the photos at home with the FastFoto, and send out only the odd items, such as film reels, MiniDV tapes without a FireWire setup, or damaged tapes.

Good to know

  • Prices were read from each store page on Sunday, October 25, 2026, before tax and shipping. Sale prices can end at any time.
  • Micro Center pickup-only items (Dell PC, Seagate drive) need a Micro Center store nearby. The Dell has a 15-day return window there.
  • The FastFoto takes prints up to 8x10 inches and thinner than 0.3 mm. Thicker or fragile items go on the flatbed.
  • The Elgato's reviews on its own site average 2.7 out of 5 (56 reviews). It is picked for its simple guided software; buy where returns are easy. Badly worn tapes may need a professional transfer.
  • Lenovo's own spec sheet lists one low-profile PCIe slot in this Lenovo model, and the StarTech FireWire card comes with a low-profile bracket, so MiniDV should work on either computer. Confirm the fit before buying; the Dell has more room.
  • FireWire on Windows 11 usually works with the built-in driver, but results vary by PC; test with an unimportant tape first.
  • An HP OmniDesk was considered but its price did not show on HP's page, so it is left out.
